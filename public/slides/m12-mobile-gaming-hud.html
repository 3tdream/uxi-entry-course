Every element on the HUD earns its rent · anatomy for a mobile game
Player state · top-left

Avatar + level, then HP / MP / XP bars. Always in the corner nearest the eye's default landing spot.

Genshin Impact, Diablo Immortal, Wild Rift

Objective · top-center

One-line "what am I doing right now?" with a progress fraction. Highest-value real estate.

Call of Duty Mobile, PUBG, Fortnite

Minimap + resources · top-right

Where am I, where are they, how much currency. Never centered — always a glance.

Clash of Clans, Pokémon GO, Fortnite

Virtual joystick · thumb zone left

Placed where the left thumb rests. Semi-transparent — the game is more important than the control.

Genshin Impact, CoD Mobile, Wild Rift

Action cluster · thumb zone right

Radial layout — primary action biggest, secondary smaller. Cooldowns visible under each. Fitts' Law for thumbs.

Wild Rift, Mobile Legends, Warframe M

Quickbar · bottom-center

Consumables and skill slots. Highlighted when ready, dimmed on cooldown. Optional — off if it clutters.

Diablo Immortal, Warframe Mobile, Alien Isolation

Rule for a HUD: every element must be readable at 60 fps while the eye is on the action. Toggle any of them off — the moment one feels missed, you've found what earns its rent.
