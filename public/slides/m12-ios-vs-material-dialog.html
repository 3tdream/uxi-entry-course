iOS (HIG) philosophy

  • Emphasis on the destructive action — red + bold Delete.
  • Two side-by-side buttons — Cancel on the left, action on the right, equal width.
  • Translucent surface with blur behind. No icon. Copy is short.

Material 3 philosophy

  • Equal weight for both actions — text buttons, no color-coding of danger.
  • Icon at the top for topical context, actions grouped bottom-right.
  • Longer copy allowed. The dialog is a modal surface, not a translucent overlay.
