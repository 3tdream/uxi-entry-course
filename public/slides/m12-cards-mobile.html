Section 1 · Card Grid

Uniform cards in a 2-column layout — same anatomy repeated (image → title → sub → price + rating). The uniformity is the point: the eye scans down the price column in one glance.

Use for: catalogs, portfolios, product listings. Real-world: Shopify, Etsy, Airbnb search results.

Section 2 · Card Carousel

Horizontal scroll with scroll-snap so each card locks into place. Best for "here are 6 more like the one you're looking at" — recommendations, related items, editorial rows.

Use for: recommendations, related content, editorial rows. Real-world: Netflix, Spotify, YouTube, App Store.

Section 3 · Elevated Card

Single hero card with a strong box-shadow that deepens on tap, plus contributor avatars and a primary CTA. It's a full unit of content — cover, body, actions.

Use for: featured articles, hero content, standalone announcements. Real-world: Medium featured, Product Hunt of the day.

Same anatomy scales up and down — a card is a container that stays uniform inside a set. Break uniformity only for a hero card, and only once per scroll.
