Under the hood — 2-proportion z-test
n = (zα/2·√(2·p̄·(1−p̄)) + zβ·√(p₁·(1−p₁) + p₂·(1−p₂)))² / (p₂ − p₁)²
where p₁ = baseline, p₂ = baseline × (1 + MDE), zα/2 for 95% = 1.96, zβ for 80% power = 0.84.

Power ↑ from 80% to 90% adds roughly +35% sample. Significance ↑ from 95% to 99% adds another +70%. Halving the MDE multiplies the sample by ~4×.

Try MDE 5% on a 2% baseline at 95/80 — that's why most "small" tests need weeks of traffic.
