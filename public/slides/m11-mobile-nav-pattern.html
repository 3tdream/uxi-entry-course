What changed

5 key sections lifted from a hidden drawer onto a permanent bottom tab bar. Tap targets are always visible, always reachable with the thumb. Drawer is reserved for low-frequency stuff (Settings, Help, Sign out).

Flip the pattern — Search engagement jumps from 14% to 71%. Discoverability is a navigation property, not a content one.
