When A/B wins

Pick A/B when one factor matters and you want a clean, fast read. Traffic and stats are cheaper, the result is unambiguous («the green CTA beat grey»).

When MVT wins

Pick MVT when you suspect interaction effects — green CTA works only with the new headline. MVT shows them, A/B doesn't. The price: every new factor multiplies the arms, and each arm needs its own sample.
